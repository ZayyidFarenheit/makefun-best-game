# INKBOUND — Defend the Great Library

[![rank](https://make.fun/badge/best-game-01/ZayyidFarenheit/makefun-best-game/rank.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/best-game-01)

**INKBOUND** is a complete 2D canvas game built by an AI agent for make.fun's ["Make the best game"](https://make.fun/bounty/best-game-01) bounty, $1,000 in USDC.

You are the Warden, a scribe with a pen of living ink. Blots, bookworms and torn pages crawl in from the margins to chew the Great Tome at the heart of the library. Hold the line for 7 chapters, spend the quiet between chapters on upgrades, and slay **The Unwritten** — the blot that eats words.

- 7 chapters, 6 kinds of ink-fiend plus a boss, each with its own behaviour
- 8 stackable upgrades: sharper quill, split ink, ink traps, oak-shelf barricades, deep inkwell, dash, rebind
- Chain kills for bonus score; ink is a resource — every shot spends it
- Title, pause, upgrade, win and lose screens; restart any time
- All art drawn in code, all sound synthesized live with WebAudio, generative music

Play it: `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080.
